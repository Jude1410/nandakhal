Who We Are

Nandakhal Youth Foundation (NYF) is a dynamic, community-driven organization dedicated to nurturing the next generation of leaders in the Nandakhal Parish. We focus on holistic development through sports, education, cultural activities, and social responsibility.

Our mission is to create opportunities for youth to discover their potential, build meaningful skills, and contribute positively to society. We believe in the transformative power of sports, education, and community service.

With a strong board of 8 trustees and a commitment to serving 15-16 villages with approximately 12,000 residents, NYF has established itself as a beacon of hope and development in the region.

Detailed Programs & Benefits

Explore what each wing offers in detail

Sports Development

Our sports program is designed to identify and nurture talent from the grassroots level while promoting fitness and a healthy lifestyle among all age groups.

Village Leagues

Regular sporting events organized at village level to promote participation and foster friendly competition.

Professional Coaching

Expert coaches provide training in football, volleyball, cricket, and athletics to develop technical skills.

Equipment Support

We maintain an equipment bank to support financially weak but talented players in pursuing their sports dreams.

Anti-Drug Initiatives

Sports and fitness programs encourage a drug-free lifestyle and promote overall wellness.

Skill & Career Development

We empower youth with practical skills and career guidance to secure better employment opportunities and entrepreneurial ventures.

Resume & Interview Training

Workshops to help youth prepare professional resumes and interview skills for job success.

Digital Skills

Training programs in digital literacy, online safety, and technology skills needed in the modern job market.

Competitive Exams

Guidance and preparation for competitive exams like banking, civil services, and professional certifications.

Entrepreneurship

Training and mentoring for youth interested in starting their own businesses and ventures.

Vision & Mission

Our commitment to building a better future

🎯 Our Vision

To build disciplined, skilled, and socially responsible youth who are equipped to lead, inspire, and create positive change in society. We envision a Nandakhal Parish where every young person has access to opportunities for sports, education, and personal development.

💪 Our Mission

  • Promote grassroots sports (football, volleyball, cricket, athletics)
  • Organize annual tournaments, leagues, and inter-parish competitions
  • Provide professional coaching and personalized mentoring
  • Support talented but financially weak players to achieve their dreams
  • Conduct personality development and career guidance workshops
  • Encourage fitness, wellness, and a drug-free lifestyle
  • Maintain and expand the green belt across Vasai villages
  • Foster cultural exchange and social responsibility
